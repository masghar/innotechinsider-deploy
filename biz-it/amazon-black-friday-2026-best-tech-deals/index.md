## Pre-Checkout Verification

Before checking out, verify that third-party storefronts are not masking used inventory as brand-new units. Confirm the listing states "Ships from Amazon, Sold by Amazon" or originates directly from the brand’s verified manufacturer store.

Keep in mind that payment-card warranty protections frequently depend on purchasing from authorized dealers. Taking two minutes to verify serial numbers upon arrival and running crystal-disk diagnostic passes on storage drives ensures your Black Friday acquisition delivers functional utility long after the sales event concludes.
