# Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali becomes first foreign astronaut on Tiangong

> Shenzhou-24 lifted off on November 25 with Muhammad Zeeshan Ali aboard. He is the first foreign astronaut to enter China's space station.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Science · Nov 27, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/science/pakistan-zeeshan-ali-shenzhou-24-first-foreign-astronaut-tiangong/

Pakistan has put its first astronaut into orbit. Muhammad Zeeshan Ali launched aboard China's Shenzhou-24 spacecraft on November 25, 2026, and is the first foreign astronaut to board the Tiangong space station, according to [Space.com](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/human-spaceflight/pakistani-astronaut-becomes-1st-international-visitor-to-chinas-tiangong-space-station-photos).

## The launch

A Long March 2F rocket carried the crew from the Jiuquan Satellite Launch Center at 8:18 a.m. Beijing time on November 25, which was 7:18 p.m. EST on November 24. Tang Hongbo commands the mission, on what [SpacePolicyOnline](https://spacepolicyonline.com/news/first-pakistani-astronaut-enroute-to-tiangong/) says is his third trip to the station. Ali flies as a payload specialist, the role China's space agency assigned him, rather than as a pilot or flight engineer. He is 40 and previously flew as a pilot in Pakistan's armed forces.

The spacecraft reached the station a little over six hours after liftoff. The outlets that covered it give slightly different clock times for docking and hatch opening, so treat the exact minute as unsettled. What they agree on is the sequence: launch, a same-day rendezvous, and Ali stepping into Tiangong.

## A short visit

Ali is not staying for a full rotation. Space.com reports he will come home in about a week, returning with two of the three astronauts from the earlier Shenzhou-23 mission, while [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/2046753) puts his stay at five to ten days. Tang and the other Shenzhou-24 crew member settle in for the usual six months. One Shenzhou-23 astronaut, Zhu Yangzhu, stays on after the handover and is on track for a record stay in orbit for a Chinese astronaut.

## What the record does and doesn't mean

The milestones are specific, and worth stating carefully. Ali is the first foreigner to fly in China's crewed space program and the first to enter Tiangong. Space.com notes he is not the first Pakistani to leave the ground for space, since Namira Salim flew a suborbital Virgin Galactic tourist flight in 2023. Dawn describes him as the first Pakistani in orbit, which is the accurate way to frame it.

## What he said, and what officials said

Quoted by Dawn from a CCTV report, Ali said in Chinese: "As the first foreign crew member to board the China Space Station, I am incredibly excited." At a press briefing before launch he said, "This mission is not a finish line; it is a launch pad." Chinese President Xi Jinping called the station "an ark of peace, friendship and cooperation" in a letter to Pakistan's President Asif Ali Zardari, according to Dawn, and Pakistani leaders framed the flight as a milestone in the two countries' relationship.

## What comes next

The visit's value will be judged by what Ali does aboard. Pakistan's space agency SUPARCO has described experiments on microalgae and seeds, which we cover separately. The first results are likely to come after he lands, and the open question is whether this flight turns into a regular program for foreign crew members or stays a one-time first.
