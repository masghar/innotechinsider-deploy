About this tracker

This is InnotechInsider's own, original count — not a copy of any other layoffs database. A daily pipeline scans tech-news coverage for genuine layoff announcements, and for each candidate report, extracts only facts that are explicitly stated in the article text: the company's real name and a specific, stated number of jobs cut. Nothing is estimated. A headline reporting "hundreds" of job cuts or an "undisclosed number" is deliberately left out of the total rather than guessed at.

Because of that standard, this tracker will show a smaller total than trackers that have been running for years or that count unconfirmed/rumored cuts — that's the tradeoff for not inventing a number and not republishing someone else's dataset. Tracking started 2026-01-29; this is a running count from that date forward, not a historical archive.

Looking for longer historical depth? TrueUp's layoffs tracker and Layoffs.fyi have been running for years and cover a wider date range — we link to them rather than reproduce their data.

Spot an error, or a source that doesn't hold up? Let us know — see the Corrections Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Where does this data come from?

Every event on this page is pulled from real news coverage of a specific, named company confirming a specific, stated number of job cuts — never an estimate. Each row links to the original source article. InnotechInsider does not copy or republish any other tracker's dataset.

How often is this updated?

Automatically, once a day, as new verified layoff reports are found.

Why doesn't this match other layoffs trackers' totals?

This tracker only counts layoffs confirmed with a specific number, and only from 2026-01-29 forward — it is not a historical archive, and it intentionally excludes vague reports ("hundreds," "an undisclosed number") rather than estimate a figure. Older or longer-running trackers will show larger totals.
