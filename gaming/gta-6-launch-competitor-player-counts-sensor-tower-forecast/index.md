# GTA VI's launch has not yet dented Steam or Roblox, early data shows

> Sensor Tower forecast a 10% drop in rival games' daily players. Early Steam and Roblox data barely moved, but PC isn't getting GTA VI, so it settles little.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 23, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-launch-competitor-player-counts-sensor-tower-forecast/

Before GTA VI shipped, the most-repeated prediction was that it would hollow out everything else. [Sensor Tower's engagement report](https://sensortower.com/report/gta-vi-impact-on-consumer-engagement) estimated that daily players on games other than GTA VI would fall more than 10%, and flagged Fortnite as the most exposed because 42% of its users also play GTA V. Four days after launch, the first public numbers have not obviously backed that up, though most of them come with serious limits.

## What the early data shows

Two third-party trackers cited by [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175382-gta-6-launch-barely-denting-steam-and-roblox-player-counts.html) put Steam's peak concurrent users at 11.4 million on November 20, against 11.7 million a week earlier. Roblox is reported at 9.6 million players on November 19, against 9.7 million on November 12. Both are small dips, roughly in the range of a normal week-to-week wobble.

Genvid CEO Jacob Navok told the outlet he had expected a significant drop in Steam and Fortnite numbers and said it never came. His reading is that many GTA VI buyers were lapsed players pulled back into gaming, and that most bought the $100 edition. That is his interpretation, not a measurement.

## Why Steam proves little

The obvious caveat is that GTA VI is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. A PC player has no way to swap Steam time for GTA VI. Steam holding steady says almost nothing about the forecast, which covered all platforms. Roblox, which skews toward younger players, is a similarly weak test.

The console side is the better place to look, and the numbers there are striking. Circana analyst Mat Piscatella's launch-day data, as [relayed by GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/circana-says-gta-6-attracted-casual-gamers-and-had-them-play-it-for-8-hours-a-day-d5e9), says 61% of active US PS5 players and 56% of active Xbox players worldwide played GTA VI on November 19. Average playtime among them was 513 minutes on PS5 and 494 minutes on Xbox. Fortnite, the second most-played game that day, reached 10% of active players.

Those figures are not directly comparable. The PS5 number is US-only and the Xbox one is global, and both count anyone who used a console that day. GTABoom also describes this as a single day, not a trend.

## The honest read

Eight hours of play from more than half of active console owners means a lot of attention went somewhere on launch day. Fortnite reaching 10% of active players in the same window shows it was still being played. What nobody has published yet is Fortnite's own player count, or Call of Duty's, or EA Sports FC's, so the 10% forecast is still neither confirmed nor refuted.

Watch the second and third weeks, when novelty fades and schedules return to normal. Launch-day binges end, and the real test of the Sensor Tower estimate is whether console players return to their old games once the first story missions are done. Until Epic, Activision or EA release numbers, or a tracker with console-level data does, treat both the "GTA VI crushed everyone" and the "nothing happened" narratives as premature.
