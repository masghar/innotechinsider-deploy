# GTA VI launches today on PS5 and Xbox, and PC players still wait

> Grand Theft Auto VI is out on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has given PC players no date at all.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 18, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-launch-day-ps5-xbox-no-pc-date/

Grand Theft Auto VI is available today. Rockstar Games set the release for [Thursday, November 19, 2026](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026), and the date has held since the studio last moved it a year ago.

## What is confirmed

The game ships on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar's support information, as relayed by launch-date trackers, says it unlocks at midnight local time on November 19 in every region, so players in Asia and Oceania got it first and the Americas last. Preloads opened on November 10, and the install asks for [up to 212GB of free storage](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/sony-declares-gta-6-plays-best-ps5-massive-212gb-pre-loads-begin-ahead-official-launch-1831662), which is enough to force a purge of the library on a base PS5.

That is the whole confirmed list. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date for one. Anyone telling you PC is "coming soon" is guessing.

## How long the wait was

The path to today was slow. The game was first aimed at 2025, then moved to May 26, 2026, and in November 2025 Rockstar [pushed it to November 19, 2026](https://rockstarreport.com/features/gta-6-release-date-tracker) to spend more time on polish. Plenty of observers assumed another slip was coming. It did not.

## The PC gap

Rockstar has a habit of staging its releases. Grand Theft Auto V reached PC more than a year after consoles, and Red Dead Redemption 2 took about a year as well. Nothing Rockstar has said about GTA VI breaks that pattern, but nothing confirms it either. For now, the only way to play is on a console.

## What to do on launch day

Expect the servers to be busy. Launch-week traffic for a game this size tends to strain matchmaking and login for the first hours, so the sensible move is to start the single-player campaign and leave online for later in the week. If your storage is tight, clear space before the unlock rather than after it.

The bigger questions, such as how the game performs and what it means for Take-Two's year, are covered in our other launch-week pieces. For today, the news is simple: the game is out, it is console-only, and PC players are the ones left waiting.
