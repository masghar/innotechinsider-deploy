# Rockstar shipped two GTA VI updates before launch with no patch notes

> Two mandatory downloads landed before GTA VI unlocked, and Rockstar explained neither. The file sizes hint at what changed, but they can't say why or what players will notice.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 21, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-two-prelaunch-updates-no-patch-notes-rockstar/

Players who wanted to be in Vice City at midnight on November 19 first had to download two updates that Rockstar never explained. As of the latest coverage we could find, the studio has not published patch notes for either one, which leaves the community reading file sizes like tea leaves.

## Two updates in four days

The first, version 01.002.000, arrived on November 13, six days before launch. [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/330664/20261113/gta-6s-mystery-update-already-your-console-what-story-data-files-reveal.htm) describes it as a silent, mandatory download of roughly 1 GB on top of the 212 GB base install, rolled out in stages: about half of players got it first and the rest roughly 11 hours later.

The second, 01.002.001, followed on November 16. [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-second-day-one-patch-ps5-xbox/) puts it at about 602.5 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S. The Xbox figure comes from dataminers, not from Rockstar or Microsoft, so treat it as reported rather than confirmed. On PS5, Sony staged the release in three waves 12 hours apart, with the last reaching everyone on November 17.

## What the file data does and doesn't show

Tech Times, citing the tracking account iGrandTheftAuto, reports that two packages changed in the first update: a story content file of about 15.75 MiB and an Americas level file of about 35.75 MiB. Audio, cinematic and texture packages reportedly stayed the same size.

The publication's reading is that the labels suggest changes to narrative scripts, mission logic or open-world geometry. That is an inference by the author, and a package name is a thin basis for it. Size changes tell you something moved. They don't tell you whether it was a bug fix, a balance tweak or anything players would notice.

## Sony added its own requirement

The game download wasn't the only gate on PlayStation. Tech Times says Sony shipped system firmware 14.20 on October 21 and 14.22 on November 2, the latter at 1.267 GB, and that GTA VI's minimum PS5 firmware reportedly rose from 14.10 to 14.22, per PlayStation Game Size tracking. We haven't confirmed that figure with Sony.

## Why the silence matters

Rockstar also hasn't linked the first update to the map leak that Tech Times says surfaced the same week, and nothing we found suggests it has addressed it. We can't say whether the two are connected, and nobody outside Rockstar can.

The silence has a cost. One of the most-anticipated game launches in years shipped with an unexplained build change days before release, and players on a rolling midnight unlock had no way to know what they were installing. Rockstar's GTA V support pages carry detailed title-update notes, so the studio knows how to write them. Publishing even a few lines for 01.002.000 and 01.002.001 would have cost almost nothing and shut down a week of speculation.

One caveat on our reporting: our search found no post-launch patch notes, but Rockstar's support site is the authoritative place to check, and a notes page may have appeared after the coverage we saw. If one has, the argument above gets weaker. If not, the first public changelog for the biggest launch of the year is still owed.
