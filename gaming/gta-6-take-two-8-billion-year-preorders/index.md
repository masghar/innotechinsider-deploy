# What GTA VI's launch means for Take-Two's $8 billion year

> Take-Two's whole fiscal year hangs on one release. Its outlook is $8 to $8.2 billion in net bookings, and it has not raised that number yet.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 18, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-take-two-8-billion-year-preorders/

Take-Two Interactive reported its fiscal second quarter on November 9, ten days before Grand Theft Auto VI went on sale. The quarter itself was weak. The outlook around it was not.

## A soft quarter

According to the company's [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000946581/000162828026072175/ttwo2q27earningsrelease.htm), net bookings fell 18% to $1.60 billion from $1.96 billion a year earlier. [GamesBeat noted](https://gamesbeat.com/take-two-reports-bookings-for-fiscal-q2-of-1-6b-down-18-and-below-expectations/) that the result was also just under the $1.62 to $1.67 billion range the company had guided to. A publisher with a big release a few weeks off tends to see the quarter before it soften, as sales of older titles flatten and marketing spend rises, so a dip was not a surprise.

## The number that matters

Take-Two kept its fiscal 2027 outlook for net bookings at $8.0 to $8.2 billion. CEO Strauss Zelnick said, "We are reiterating our outlook for Fiscal 2027 Net Bookings of $8 to $8.2 billion," and tied the figure to pre-order strength. Call summaries say management declined to raise the range until it sees how the launch performs.

That choice is worth reading carefully. A company that says pre-orders keep setting new highs could have lifted its guidance and chose not to. Holding the range leaves room to beat it, which is a safer position for a publisher whose year depends on one title.

## What nobody knows yet

Take-Two has not published a pre-order total or a revenue figure. What exists are third-party estimates. Sensor Tower's number, [reported as about $1.65 billion across roughly 17.1 million units](https://vistapglobal.com/take-twos-gta-vi-arrives-with-1-65-billion-in-estimated-preorders-the-bigger-prize-comes-after-launch-msft-sony-ttwo/), is the one cited most often, and other outlets circulate lower figures near $1.5 billion. None of these are company results, and they cover spending tracked on storefronts, not Take-Two's recognized revenue.

Reports of the next quarter's guidance also conflict. One gives $3.35 to $3.5 billion for the quarter ending December 31 and another gives $3.27 to $3.42 billion, so check the company's release before relying on either.

## What to watch

The first hard data will come with Take-Two's next report, which will show how much of the launch landed in the December quarter. Until then, the useful read is the one Zelnick has already given: the company is betting the year on a single game, and so far that game has the reviews to back it.
