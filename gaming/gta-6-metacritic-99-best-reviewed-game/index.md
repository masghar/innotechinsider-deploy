# GTA VI scores a 99 on Metacritic, matching Ocarina of Time's record

> Critics have almost uniformly praised Grand Theft Auto VI, with IGN and GameSpot both giving it a 10. The complaints are about pacing, restrictive missions, and 30fps.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 18, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-metacritic-99-best-reviewed-game/

Grand Theft Auto VI arrived with the strongest critical reception a big-budget game has had in years. Its first review wave put it at [99 on Metacritic](https://thenextweb.com/news/gta-vi-metacritic-99-reviews-30fps-launch), level with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest score the site has recorded. [Bloomberg called it the best-reviewed video game ever](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-18/-grand-theft-auto-vi-becomes-the-best-reviewed-video-game-ever).

## The numbers, with a caveat

Outlets disagree slightly on the details. Reports put the Metacritic review count anywhere from about 145 to 153, and OpenCritic is cited at 98 in most places and 99 in one. Reviews were still coming in on November 18, and a single middling score could break the tie with Ocarina of Time in either direction. Treat 99 as the headline and the exact count as unsettled.

Individual verdicts are strong. IGN and GameSpot each gave the game a 10, and VGC gave it 100 out of 100. Roundups note that OpenCritic shows every critic recommending it, with no mixed or negative reviews in the first wave.

## What critics pushed back on

Even a 99 comes with notes. Roundups of the reviews, including [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/gta/news/gta-6-review-roundup), point to a few recurring complaints:

- Some campaign missions are restrictive, steering players down a single approach.
- Pacing sags in places.
- Controls have occasional rough spots.
- The game targets 30 frames per second on console, which several reviewers called disappointing.

None of these dented the scores much, which says something about how much the rest of the package impressed people.

## What a score like this does and doesn't tell you

Review aggregates measure critics, not players, and they are taken in the first days, before most of the game's online features have faced real crowds. A 99 shows that reviewers who got early access were won over. It does not predict how the game holds up after a month of play, or how the community reacts once the novelty fades.

It also arrives with a lot riding on it. Take-Two has built its financial year around this release, which we look at separately. Critics have now given the game about the best start it could ask for. Players get their say starting today.
