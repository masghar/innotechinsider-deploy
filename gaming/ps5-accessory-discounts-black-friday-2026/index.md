# PS5 Accessory Prices Drop Early Ahead of Black Friday Rush

> Major retailers have rolled out early Black Friday discounts on PlayStation 5 gear, shaving prices on DualSense controllers, headsets, and storage expansions.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 16, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/ps5-accessory-discounts-black-friday-2026/

> **TL;DR** Major electronics retailers and Sony's direct storefront have rolled out early Black Friday price cuts across the PlayStation 5 accessory lineup, marking down DualSense controllers, official audio gear, and compatible storage drives nearly two weeks before Thanksgiving.

Holiday promotional schedules keep sliding earlier into November. As of Monday, major retailers including Amazon, Best Buy, and Target—alongside Sony's own direct sales channel—have slashed prices across the primary range of PlayStation 5 peripherals. Rather than holding inventory for Thanksgiving weekend, storefronts are pushing substantial discounts to lock in hardware ecosystem sales ahead of the seasonal peak.

The discounts span essential hardware accessories, ranging from standard and special-edition controllers to the platform's proprietary headsets. For players who picked up hardware during recent console revisions or are looking to equip local multiplayer setups, the pricing reflects some of the lowest recorded entry points of the year.

## The Standout Markdowns

Standard DualSense wireless controllers, which typically retail between $75 and $80 depending on the colorway, have dropped across most authorized outlets to around $55. Color variants—including the Midnight Black, Cosmic Red, and Deep Earth metallic collections—are matched at the same discount tier at participating retailers.

Sony’s mid-tier and high-end audio options are also seeing early promotional movement. The Pulse Elite wireless headset, which incorporates planar magnetic drivers derived from Sony's acquisition of Audeze, has dipped under its standard $150 threshold at select storefronts. While third-party controller alternatives have proliferated across the [gaming](/gaming/) sector, first-party Sony peripherals still hold distinct software-level integration advantages on both the standard PS5 and the PS5 Pro.

Discounts on premium peripherals like the DualSense Edge are comparatively modest, typically shaving $20 off the list price, but represent rare official cuts for enthusiast-focused hardware that rarely sees promotional pricing outside late November.

## Storage and Peripheral Add-Ons Follow Suit

The discounting extends beyond Sony-branded plastic to the broader hardware stack required for modern console gaming. Internal storage expansion drives—specifically Gen4 M.2 NVMe SSDs that meet [Sony's official PS5 drive requirements](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/ps5-install-m2-ssd/) of at least 5,500 MB/s sequential read speeds—have simultaneously dropped across several memory vendors.

Pre-heatsink models from Western Digital (WD_Black SN850X) and Samsung (990 Pro) are seeing price adjustments between 15% and 25% off their standard retail listings. With game installation footprints frequently topping 100 gigabytes per AAA release, internal storage remains the most frequently paired purchase with new console hardware.

Hardware peripherals that double as PC accessories are also showing increased promotional activity. The [PlayStation VR2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_VR2), which expanded its utility following the rollout of Sony's official PC adapter, has returned to promotional pricing tiers reminiscent of late-summer sales events. As hardware manufacturers manage broader channel supply in the [biz it](/biz-it/) logistics chain, moving peripheral volume ahead of December shipping deadlines has become a standard distributor playbook.

## Evaluating the Holiday Buying Window

For prospective buyers weighing whether to pull the trigger immediately or wait for the official Black Friday date on November 27, historical promotional patterns offer clarity. First-party accessory discounts on standard controllers and headsets rarely deepen further during Cyber Week; instead, retailers typically shift their emphasis toward game software bundles, digital storefront credit, and full console packaging.

Most major retailers have also initiated extended holiday return windows, allowing hardware purchases made in mid-November to be returned or price-matched through early January 2027. Shoppers prioritizing specific controller colors or manufacturer-heatsink SSD configurations face higher risks of stockouts than they do of missing a dramatically steeper discount later in the month.
