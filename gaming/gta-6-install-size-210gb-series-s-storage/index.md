# GTA VI's 212 GB install eats most of a 512 GB Series S

> Rockstar says GTA VI needs up to 212 GB of free space. On a 512 GB Xbox Series S, that math leaves little room for anything else.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 21, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-install-size-210gb-series-s-storage/

The number that matters most for a lot of GTA VI buyers this weekend is not a frame rate or a review score. It is up to 212 GB, the free space [Rockstar says](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10) the game needs, which is close to the 210 GB figure that circulated for weeks beforehand.

## Where the number came from

The number traces back to the PlayStation Game Size account on X, which tracks entries in Sony's database. [IBTimes reports](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/gta-vi-210gb-download-xbox-series-s-storage-1828486) the account posted it on 27 October 2026 for the PS5 Standard Edition, and that Insider Gaming corroborated it. [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/tech-news/mustafa-mahmoud/grand-theft-auto-vi-game-file-size-revealed/) also cites the account and says the Xbox size is unknown.

That early figure was a tracker's entry, and repeated write-ups of one post counted as one source, not several. Rockstar's own pre-load announcement later put the requirement at up to 212 GB, and the exact total can vary by device, so check your own console's download screen.

## What it does to a Series S

The Xbox side is where the arithmetic gets uncomfortable, and it is partly hypothetical. Rockstar's figure is a maximum, and the coverage we reviewed gave no separate Xbox number. If the Xbox build lands near that size, the arithmetic IBTimes ran for 210 GB holds roughly the same: 212 GB would take about 58% of the roughly 365 GB usable on the original 512 GB Series S. That leaves about 155 GB for the system's other games, captures and updates.

It is a real constraint for the cheapest current Xbox. One large open-world game can crowd out a library. A 1 TB Series S exists, which would ease the squeeze, but plenty of owners bought the base model precisely because it was cheap.

## Updates add to the total

The base install is not the whole bill. Rockstar shipped a first update on 13 November and a second on 16 November, which Of Zen and Computing puts at 602.5 MB on PS5 and 1.59 GB on Xbox Series X|S. Those are small next to 212 GB, but Rockstar has published no patch notes for either, so there is no way to tell from the outside what changed. Our earlier piece on [the two unannotated pre-launch updates](/gaming/gta-6-two-prelaunch-updates-no-patch-notes-rockstar/) covers that gap.

## What to do before you hit install

- Check free space on the console itself rather than trusting any tracker. The store page or download screen is the only number that counts.
- Budget extra headroom for updates. A full drive can stall or fail a large download.
- Move or delete games you are not playing before the download starts. Reinstalling them later costs bandwidth, not money.
- If you are on a 512 GB console, decide now which games you are willing to evict.

The larger point is that a launch of this size demands console storage on the scale of a small PC library. Whatever the final figure turns out to be, the first thing many players will do with GTA VI is delete something else.
