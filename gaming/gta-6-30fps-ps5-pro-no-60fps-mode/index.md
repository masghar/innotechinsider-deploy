# GTA VI runs at 30fps on every console, PS5 Pro included

> Rockstar's target is 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S, with no 60fps mode. The Pro gets sharper visuals instead.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Gaming · Nov 18, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/gaming/gta-6-30fps-ps5-pro-no-60fps-mode/

Grand Theft Auto VI will run at 30 frames per second on PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has said there is no 60fps mode, and [Kotaku reports](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-wont-get-a-60-fps-mode-even-on-ps5-pro-because-its-not-powerful-enough-2000755979) the studio confirmed that this will not change for the Pro either.

## What the PS5 Pro actually gets

The Pro does not buy a faster game. According to [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-ps5-pro-features-revealed-30fps-target-on-both-consoles), its extra power goes to image quality: ray-traced shadows, and added clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution, Sony's upscaler. So the choice between the two PlayStation models is about how the game looks, not how smoothly it moves.

## Why 30fps

Rockstar has not, to our knowledge, published a technical breakdown, so the reasoning is inference. Open-world games that simulate dense crowds, traffic, and weather in real time are heavy on the CPU, and the current consoles' processors are modest. Holding a locked 30 leaves room for the simulation depth the series is known for. Reviewers noted the cap, and several called it disappointing, though it did not stop the game reaching [a 99 on Metacritic](https://thenextweb.com/news/gta-vi-metacritic-99-reviews-30fps-launch).

## Series S and the install

Details for the Series S are thin. Rockstar has not published its native resolution or the compromises it makes, so any specific figures you see are speculation. One guide listing a 60fps mode with exact resolutions contradicts the official position, and should be ignored.

The install is large. Rockstar says the game needs up to 212GB of free space, with preloads open since November 10. On a console with a 1TB drive that is a substantial share of the disk once system files are counted.

## What to do

If you own a Pro, enable its enhanced mode for the shadows and clarity. If you own a base console, you are not missing a smoother option, because none exists. And if you were waiting for a high-frame-rate version, that means PC, where Rockstar has announced nothing yet.
