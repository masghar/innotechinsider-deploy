# Early Apple Black Friday Deals Hit Major Retailers: What to Buy Now

> Major retailers have launched early Black Friday discounts on AirPods, MacBooks, and Apple Watches. Here is where the real value lies before the holiday rush.

By InnotechInsider Staff · Apple · Nov 21, 2026
Canonical URL: https://innotechinsider.com/apple/apple-black-friday-deals-guide-2026/

> **TL;DR** Retailers like Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart have launched their holiday price cuts a full week ahead of Black Friday, offering steep discounts on AirPods, iPads, and M-series MacBooks while Apple sticks to its traditional gift-card bundles.

The calendar still says mid-November, but the retail sector has effectively discarded the traditional post-Thanksgiving timetable. With Black Friday landing on November 27, 2026, the major third-party retailers have already kicked off their primary sales pushes.

For shoppers eyeing Apple hardware, the dynamic remains distinctly two-tiered. Apple itself maintains fixed hardware prices and relies on promotional gift card incentives during its official shopping event, as documented on the [Apple retail portal](https://www.apple.com/shop). Third-party vendors like Amazon, Best Buy, and Target, however, are aggressively cutting the direct checkout price to capture early holiday volume.

Navigating these early promotions requires separating genuine clearance bargains from modest price trimmings. Some categories offer near-record pricing, while others feature older silicon that might not justify the checkout total.

## AirPods: Clean Cuts on Audio Hardware

## The Mac Transition: M2 and M3 Clearance vs. M4 Value

Laptops represent the largest raw dollar savings this week. Retailers are using early Black Friday events to clear remaining warehouse inventory of M2 and M3-generation MacBook Air models, making entry-level macOS machines unusually affordable.

The base 13-inch MacBook Air with 16GB of unified memory—now the standard baseline across the catalog—has dropped below $850 at several major outlets. Buyers should avoid any older 8GB inventory lingering at steep apparent discounts, as modern local workloads and background processes quickly bottleneck that footprint.

As compute demands evolve alongside local [future tech](/future-tech/) personal processing models, memory headroom matters more than a marginal jump in single-core CPU speeds. An M3 MacBook Air configured with adequate unified memory provides far better longevity than a base M4 model purchased at full retail price simply for the newer processor label.

For power users, previous-generation MacBook Pro models with Pro and Max silicon are seeing cuts between $300 and $500. Unless your workflow requires the higher sustained thermal thresholds of the newest chips, these older configurations deliver workstation performance at mainstream pricing.

## Apple Watch: Clear Separation of Tiers

Wearables present a split landscape this shopping season. The Apple Watch SE remains the volume driver for budget-conscious buyers, routinely dropping near $150. However, the hardware trade-offs—lacking an always-on display, fast charging, and advanced health sensors like ECG—remain tangible.

1. **Apple Watch Series 10:** The wide-angle OLED display and slimmer chassis introduced with Series 10 have already received their first $50 to $70 discounts. It represents the sweet spot for battery life, display legibility, and sensor accuracy for general users.
2. **Apple Watch Ultra 2:** The rugged tier sees smaller percentage cuts, hovering around $719 to $749. Its appeal rests squarely on battery endurance and extreme outdoor use cases, detailed across standard fitness benchmarks via [Wikipedia's Apple Watch documentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Watch).
3. **Apple Watch SE (2nd Gen):** Deeply discounted at $149–$169, this remains an attractive purchase for kids or elderly family members configured via Family Setup, where advanced biometric monitoring is less essential.

Inventory timing on wearable colors and band configurations tends to become erratic earlier than other hardware categories, making early order placement sensible if a specific finish is required.

## Strategy: Direct Discounts Outperform Gift Cards

Unless you hold an Apple Card yielding daily cash back or specifically plan to spend hundreds of dollars on App Store subscriptions or cloud storage, third-party retail markdowns beat Apple’s direct holiday offer. Apple's upcoming shopping event will bundle store gift cards worth between $25 and $200 with select full-price items, effectively tying your savings back into the platform's billing system.

Buying directly from third-party storefronts secures immediate capital preservation at checkout. With retail price-matching policies active through December at major electronics chains, pulling the trigger on verified all-time-low prices today eliminates the risk of inventory shortages as shipping deadlines compress next month.
